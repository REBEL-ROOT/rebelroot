## Re-Imagining Digital Literature

The traditional publishing ecosystem is broken. Distribution platforms take massive cuts, enforce draconian digital rights management (DRM), and dictate content policies. Authors are left dependent, and readers lose actual ownership of the books they buy.

MOCNovel restores digital sovereignty. Built on local-first principles, it acts as a private bookshelf and a robust self-publishing portal.

## Local-First Architecture

MOCNovel loads EPUB and PDF books instantly from your device's filesystem. There are no cloud logins required to access your library, and your reading progress, annotations, and bookmarks are stored in an encrypted database directly on your hardware.

If you choose to sync your bookshelf across devices, MOCNovel leverages decentralized sync nodes with end-to-end encryption. Your encryption key is generated locally on your device, ensuring that we never see your data.

## Empowering Authors

For creators, MOCNovel offers an integrated compilation suite:

- **Clean Typography Configurations**: Generate standardized formats that scale seamlessly from small mobile screens to tablets.
- **Zero-Middleman Distribution**: Export fully verified, auditable book bundles ready for P2P distribution networks.
- **Auditable Licensing**: Retain absolute ownership over your source files and creative decisions.
