## The Root Problem of Modern Computing

In the early days of the web, computing was highly decentralized. Users ran their own servers, chose their own applications, and maintained control over their documents. Today, a handful of monopoly corporations control the platforms we use to communicate, organize, and create.

Our files are stored on central cloud clusters, our communications are monitored to compile marketing profiles, and our applications are leased to us on monthly subscriptions that can be revoked at any time. This centralization represents a serious threat to personal freedom and collective self-determination.

## Reclaiming Sovereignty Through Auditable Tools

To combat digital encapsulation, we need software built on three core pillars:

- **100% Offline Processing**: Tools that do not require cloud check-ins or server approvals to run.
- **Open and Auditable Code**: Fully open-source under copyleft licensing (GPL-3.0), allowing anyone to build, inspect, and verify the software binaries.
- **Interoperability & Open Formats**: Saving user records in standard formats (like Markdown, EPUB, and PDF) rather than proprietary database files.

By building tools like Omni Browser, MOCNovel, and MOC Office, RebelRoot aims to offer alternative, high-quality workspaces that restore computing back to the user. We believe that privacy isn't a luxury feature — it's the fundamental baseline.
